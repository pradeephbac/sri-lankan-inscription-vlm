Field Photography Guide for Sri Lankan Sites

Practical tips for capturing clear readings at caves, drip-ledges, and temple pillars.

01. Use Oblique Natural Light

Morning or late afternoon raking sunlight casts shallow shadows inside weathered Brahmi chiseling, making worn letters far more legible than harsh midday overhead sun.

02. Keep the Camera Parallel

Align your phone parallel to the stone face or paper estampage and crop closely around the inscribed lines to maximize character resolution for the model.

03. Toggle Carving Contrast

Keep Enhance Stone Carving Contrast enabled for raw rock photos; you may toggle it off if you are uploading an already high-contrast black-and-white archival rubbing.
